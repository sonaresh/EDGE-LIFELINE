# Phase 8 limitations

- All events, hospitals, resources, failures, identities, policies, and mission outcomes are
  synthetic. Results are not clinical efficacy or patient-safety evidence.
- The simulator is an accelerated mechanism comparison, not a production traffic, hardware,
  network, sensor, or human-operations model.
- The H4 admission value is a local proof-generation-plus-verification proxy. It excludes network,
  sensor, scheduling, actuator, and physical-effect latency and is not a clinical deadline.
- E9 contains a declared full-TCB compromise profile. Prevention is not claimed under full-TCB
  compromise; only post-reconnect detection and containment can be assessed.
- Pseudorandom traces are deterministic for reproducibility and do not represent prevalence.
- The independent oracle has perfect synthetic ground truth that a deployed system would not have.
- Ten seeds meet the preregistered planning floor but do not justify universal or population-wide
  inference. Confidence intervals describe only this frozen experiment.
- A favorable comparison cannot establish causality outside the paired synthetic design.
- Automated success means the experiment executed completely and reproducibly, not that H1-H9
  were confirmed. Null and unfavorable outcomes must remain in the evidence.
